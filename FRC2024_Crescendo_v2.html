GAME CONTROLS — CRESCENDO

P1 — Red · Keyboard 1

WASD = Move   Q/E = Rotate

Space = Score Note (Speaker/Amp when near zone)

P2 — Red · Controller 1

Left Stick = Move   Right Stick X = Rotate

Right Trigger / Face Btn = Score Note

P3 — Blue · Keyboard 2

↑↓←→ = Move   N/M = Rotate

RShift = Score Note

P4 — Blue · Controller 2

Left Stick = Move   Right Stick X = Rotate

Right Trigger / Face Btn = Score Note

SCORING — CRESCENDO

Drive into a Note (orange ring) to pick it up.

Drive to your Speaker and press Score: +2 pts (or +5 when Amplified)

Drive to your Amp and press Score: +1 pt. Score 2 in Amp → Amplify Speaker for 10s.
